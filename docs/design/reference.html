A calmer, louder-where-it-matters UI for the demo video

I walked all three pages and the full recorded story in mock mode at 1920×1080. The product logic is clear; the UI hides it. Text is mostly 11–12 px, the important moments sit below the fold, and seven shades of green make every block look equally important. This plan fixes that with measured specs, one layout per page, and a recording mode.

What hurts the video most

  1. Text is too small to survive compression. Body copy is 14 px and most labels are 11 px. After a 720p re-encode an 11 px Thai label renders at about 7 px, and tone marks merge.
  2. The climax is off-screen. Attacker rounds, the anonymised message and the k = 3 gate render 1,150–1,650 px down. The recorder scrolls blind with mouse.wheel(0, 450).
  3. Viewers cannot see where clicks happen. Playwright video has no cursor, and the scenario button sits 1,290 px from the inbox it changes.

What changes

  1. One measured system in soft neumorphism. One base colour with raised and inset shadow pairs, 18 tokens with tested contrast, a 9-step type scale with a 13 px floor, one icon set (Lucide, 1.75 stroke). Full width, no max-width.
  2. Each page fits one 1536×864 frame, the layout a 1080p laptop shows at 125 % scaling. Captured at 2560×1440 for a sharp 1080p edit. The bottom 136 px stays free for captions.
  3. A recording mode. ?rec=1 adds a click spotlight, moves toasts to the top and slows the pipeline reveal to a readable pace.
  4. One edit plan for desktop and LINE. A 14-shot list, four canvas layouts, captions from an .srt the recorder writes, and a LINE card that matches the web.

2 · Direction: a quiet instrument

The product watches, measures and protects. The UI should feel like a well-made instrument panel: one soft material with raised controls and inset readouts, neutral and exact, with colour only where something is protected, waiting or wrong.

Summary before detail

Each page leads with the one state that matters now: the gate count, the theme waiting for a reply, the 3 → 0 result. Detail sits under it.

Colour means state

Neutrals carry the layout. Teal means “protected / done”, amber means “waiting”, red means “identifiable”. Nothing else is coloured.

Depth means role

Raised surfaces hold things or can be pressed. Inset wells take input or hold data. Text, charts and state chips stay flat. Two depth levels per region at most.

Readable on a compressed video

No text under 13 px, no hairline that carries meaning, no tint lighter than 3:1 that has to be seen.

Leave out (generated look)

  • Sparkle or magic-wand icons for AI output. Name the model instead: qwen3.5.
  • Gradients, glass blur, glow, coloured shadows. Embossed (shadowed) text.
  • Three equal feature cards; an icon above every heading.
  • A subtitle under every heading; badges that only describe.
  • Coloured side rails on cards; radius over 24 px; inputs drawn by shadow alone.
  • Emoji or unicode glyphs as icons; negative letter-spacing on Thai.

Use instead (subject-specific)

  • Real product rules as proof: k = 3, ≤ 3 messages per person per week, metadata only.
  • Struck-through Thai markers (หนู, ค่ะ) as the signature visual.
  • Person slots for the anonymity gate, not a generic progress bar.
  • Monospace for references only: PR #123, k=3, model names.
  • Numbered steps only where order is real: the 4-step loop and the 6 privacy steps.

Type · IBM Plex Sans Thai + IBM Plex Mono

Keep IBM Plex Sans Thai: loopless, technical, and it carries Plex Latin inside the same file. Its digits share one 600-unit advance, so numbers are already tabular. Its natural line box is 1.65 em, the roomiest of the common Thai UI faces (Anuphan and Sarabun are 1.30), which suits stacked vowels and tone marks. Add IBM Plex Mono for references only.

Thai rules: tracking 0 (the current hero’s -0.025em crowds marks); body line-height 1.6–1.65, headings 1.3, never below 1.25. No Thai standard body sets these numbers, so they follow the font’s own line box and the York study’s preference for 1.5× or more. Use Arabic digits. If a long paragraph ever appears, IBM Plex Sans Thai Looped reads slightly faster in paragraphs (Typotheque/Chulalongkorn study) and shares the same metrics.

Depth · soft neumorphism with guardrails

Neumorphism draws every surface in the base colour and separates it with a light shadow (top-left) and a dark shadow (bottom-right). Its known weakness is contrast: shadows alone give about 1.4:1, below the 3:1 WCAG 1.4.11 asks of input boundaries, and soft gradients are the first thing a low-bitrate encode flattens. The guardrails below keep the look and fix both.

raised6 6 14 / −6 −6 14panels, cards
raised-sm3 3 7 / −3 −3 7buttons, chips, tabs
insetinset 3 3 7inputs, wells, gate
inset-sminset 2 2 4pressed, selected
primarysolid accentone per view
  • Inputs keep a 1 px --rule-strong border (3.15:1) inside the inset well. Shadow alone does not mark a text field.
  • The primary button is a solid teal fill (white text 6.47:1), never a shadow-only button. One per view.
  • Selected and pressed states flip to inset and change text to --accent-ink: two cues, not shadow alone.
  • Focus stays a 2 px --accent outline, 3 px offset.
  • Text, charts and state chips are flat. Never emboss text.
  • Two depth levels per region at most (raised panel → inset well → raised bubble is the limit).
  • Shadow distance ≥ 3 px, blur ≈ 2× distance, dark core ≥ 1.3:1 on base, so the edge survives a 720p encode. Export at CRF ≤ 18.
  • LINE stays flat. Flex messages have no shadows; colours and type still match.

Layout · full width

  • No max-width on page containers. Remove .workspace 1600/1700 px and .home-main 1320 px; pages fill the window.
  • Gutter clamp(16px, 2.1vw, 48px): 32 px at the 1536 recording width, 48 px on wide monitors.
  • Fluid columns. Demo: clamp(260px, 19vw, 360px) clamp(360px, 26vw, 520px) 1fr. Manager: minmax(0, 1.6fr) minmax(400px, 1fr). Home hero: minmax(0, 1fr) minmax(0, .78fr).
  • Text keeps its own measure, not the container’s: paragraphs max-width: 68ch, hero pitch 30em, theme voice rows 72ch. Wide screens get more room, not longer lines.

4.2 Phone takes and the LINE OA

  • Devices. Mint on an iPhone (she taps quick replies). Somchai on a second iPhone, or on LINE for Mac: he only receives, so the missing quick replies there do not matter.
  • Record with Control Center → Screen Recording, microphone off. iPhone files are variable frame rate: convert to 30 fps constant before editing (command in 4.6).
  • Phone state. Light mode, Do Not Disturb focus, Wi-Fi on, cellular off, charged. The same plain chat background on both phones.
  • LINE text size Large (LINE → Settings → Chats → Font size). At 912 px phone height in the edit, message text lands near 21 px instead of 18 px.
  • OA identity. Profile image is the brand mark (white bridge on #0b6a60, 640×640 PNG); display name เกรงใจบริดจ์.
  • Paste, don’t type, the long feedback. Use the exact FEEDBACK and REPLY strings from record_demo.py so the web and phone takes tell the same story.
  • Taps. iOS shows none. Add a 56 px ring (#0b6a60, 2 px, 300 ms) at each tap in the edit. On Android: Developer options → Show taps.
  • Before each take, reset the demo and check /api/health shows llm_available: true. LINE takes use the live server, not mock mode.

7 · Decisions for you

My recommendation is first in each. Say “go with the defaults” and I start Phase 0.

  1. Soft neumorphism with the section 3 guardrails (bordered inputs, solid primary, flat text and charts).

    Alternative: pure neumorphism with shadow-only inputs and buttons. It looks softer but fails WCAG 1.4.11 and loses edges in compressed video.

  2. Full width, with measure caps on text blocks.

    Alternative: full width everywhere including paragraphs, which makes 200+ character lines on a 2560 px monitor.

  3. Lay out at 1536×864 and capture at 2560×1440 (device scale 1.667).

    Alternative: keep a 1920×1080 viewport at scale 1, which needs every size about 25 % larger and leaves no room for punch-ins or split screens.

  4. Keep the teal brand, tightened to one accent.

    Alternative: move to a blue accent for a more conventional “tech” read; this loses continuity with the favicon and slides.

  5. Home primary button opens the demo console.

    Alternative: keep “ดูแดชบอร์ดหัวหน้า” as primary.

  6. Replace Chart.js with inline SVG sparklines on the manager page.

    Removes the only third-party script, so a slow CDN cannot blank a chart mid-recording.

  7. Captions stay at the bottom; layouts keep the bottom 136 px for secondary content.

    Alternative: a top caption band that covers the page-title row.

  8. Captions are added in the editor from demo.srt, not burned in.

    One style across web and phone shots, and timing can follow a voice-over. Alternative: keep burned-in captions for web shots and type phone captions by hand.

  9. Final web take with OBS (path B); Playwright video for rehearsals.

    Alternative: Playwright video only, which is simpler but blurs during scrolls.

  10. Restyle the LINE preview card to match the web.

    One backend function. Alternative: leave LINE as it is and keep the change to app/static.

  11. Editor: CapCut desktop (free, imports SRT, good Thai fonts) or DaVinci Resolve (free, precise markers). Your team’s choice; the shot list works in both.
